What it does

From box list to loading plan in a minute

Built for Amazon FBA and AWD sellers, freight forwarders and 3PL teams who need the pallet count, the container and the loading order right the first time.

Pallets to Amazon's rules

Full flat layers, no overhang, part-full layers only on top. Height and weight limits checked for every pallet and stack.

Sea containers in 3D

20', 40', 45' dry, high cube, reefer, open top and flat rack. Two floor patterns, stacking, door height and payload checks.

Air ULDs with contours

PMC, PAG, PLA pallets and LD3, LD8, LD11 containers. Tall pallets go where the contour allows, anything outside shows in red.

Floor-loaded cartons

No pallets? Plan loose cartons in walls from the front wall to the doors, with box numbers for every wall.

Sea vs air

Pallet sheets with box numbers per layer, a loading plan PDF with 3D views, Excel export, and a share link that holds the whole plan.

How it works

Three steps

No account and no upload. Your project is saved in your own browser, and you can save a copy as a file at any time.

Import your box file

Use the same Excel file you upload to Seller Central (SKU, units per box, boxes, box size and weight), or type the lines in.

Pick sea or air

Choose a container or ULD and your pallet limits. Compare the options side by side and pick the one with the fewest containers or the lowest cost.

Print and share

Print pallet sheets for the warehouse, export to Excel for your forwarder, or send a link that opens the same plan.

FAQ

Questions

Is it free? Do I need an account?

Yes, it's free and there is no account. Open the planner and start.

Where is my shipment data stored?

Only in your own browser. Nothing is sent to a server. A share link holds the plan inside the link itself, so only people you send it to can open it.

Which file can I import?

The Amazon inbound box file (.xlsx or .csv) with columns like Merchant SKU, Units per box, Number of boxes, Box length, width, height and weight. Inches/pounds and centimetres/kilograms are both read from the column headings.

Are the container and ULD sizes exact?

They are typical values. Containers vary by owner and build year, and air contours vary by aircraft type and position. Always confirm the final plan with your forwarder or airline.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. The 3D views rotate with a finger and pinch to zoom. For long shipments a laptop is easier.
